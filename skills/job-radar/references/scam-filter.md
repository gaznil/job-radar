# Scam filter

Check every posting before scoring. One **block** sign → never apply; list it in the report as
"blocked" with the reason. **Warning** signs → apply only in review-first mode, with the warning
shown.

## Block

- Asks for any fee: registration, training, security deposit, visa, medical, "processing",
  uniform, laptop.
- Asks for passport, Aadhaar, PAN or bank details before an interview or written offer.
- "Task", "like and earn", "rating", "typing", "data entry from home" jobs paid per task, or any
  job run through a Telegram or WhatsApp group.
- Overseas recruitment agent not registered on the Government of India eMigrate portal
  (https://emigrate.gov.in, list of registered Recruiting Agents). Applies to every private
  agency recruiting for the Gulf or abroad.
- Salary far above normal for the role and experience (e.g. ₹60,000/month for a fresher data
  entry job, AED 8,000 for a helper) with no company named.
- Offer letter or "selected" message without any interview.
- Visit visa offered as a work visa ("come on visit, we'll convert").

## Warning

- No company name, only a phone number or personal Gmail/Yahoo address.
- Company website missing, or the domain doesn't match the email.
- Very vague job description.
- Pressure to decide today.
- Posting copied across many sites with different company names.

## Safe signals

- Government recruiters: NORKA Roots, ODEPC, Kerala PSC, CMD Kerala, NCS, Dubai Careers.
- Posting on the company's own careers page or hiring system.
- Agency licence number shown and matching on eMigrate.

When in doubt, tell the user what you found and let them decide. Never contact a suspicious
recruiter.
