# Source pack: Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain)

`{role}` = job title. `{country}` = each country the user chose; for UAE also try
`Dubai OR "Abu Dhabi" OR Sharjah`.

Run `scam-filter.md` strictly on every Gulf posting. Fake visa and agent offers are the most
common job scam targeting Kerala.

## 1. Government recruiters (safest; check first)

| Source | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| NORKA Roots | https://norkaroots.org | Kerala government overseas recruitment (nurses, doctors, technicians; Gulf, UK, Germany). Checked Oct 2026. |
| ODEPC | https://odepc.kerala.gov.in | Kerala government overseas recruitment agency. |
| Dubai Careers | https://dubaicareers.ae | Dubai government jobs. |

These boards block automated reading; search with `"{role}" {country} naukrigulf` etc. and open
the posting, or browse in the user's Chrome.

## 4. Email-apply searches (Gulf postings often say "send CV to")

```
"{role}" {country} "send CV" OR "send your CV" OR "email your CV"
"{role}" {country} "urgently required"
"{role}" {country} "walk-in interview" Kochi OR Trivandrum OR Calicut   ← Gulf firms interviewing in Kerala
```

## Gulf CV notes

Gulf recruiters usually expect: nationality, current location, visa status, notice period,
driving licence (Indian / GCC), and languages. Add them to the tailored CV's contact block when
applying to Gulf jobs. No photo unless the posting asks for one.
