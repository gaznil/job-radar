# Onboarding interview

Ask 3–4 questions at a time, not all at once. Accept short answers. Use Malayalam if the user
writes in Malayalam. Skip anything already in their CV; confirm it instead.

## Round 1: what they want

1. What job (or jobs) are you looking for? Any titles you would also accept?
2. Where are you now, and where are you willing to work?
   (Your district / anywhere in Kerala / other Indian cities / Gulf / UK-Europe / remote)
3. Do you have a CV? Paste it or attach it. If not, no problem, I'll build one with you.

## Round 2: background (skip what the CV already answers)

4. Total experience in years, and in this exact role.
5. Education: highest qualification, field, year, and any registrations or licences
   (nursing council, driving licence: Indian / GCC, CA/CMA, etc.).
6. Key skills and tools you use (software, machines, languages spoken).
7. Your last 2–3 jobs: company, title, dates, 2–3 things you did or achieved (numbers help).

## Round 3: conditions

8. Expected salary (monthly, in ₹ or AED/SAR etc.) and the lowest you'd accept.
9. Notice period / when you can join.
10. For abroad: passport valid? Any current visa? Gulf experience? Need the employer to sponsor?
11. Anything you won't accept? (night shift, field work, sales targets, contract-only, etc.)

## Round 4: how I work for you

12. **Apply mode:**
    - **Full auto:** I submit applications scoring above your threshold myself (where possible)
      and report after.
    - **Review first (recommended to start):** I prepare everything, you reply "yes" or the
      numbers to send, then I submit.
13. **Daily limit:** default 10 applications a day. Threshold: default 75/100.
14. Connectors: check which they have and offer the missing ones one by one (Indeed, LinkedIn,
    Gmail, Claude in Chrome, Google Drive) using `connectors.md`. Also ask if they have a paid
    plan (needed for daily scheduled runs).

## After the interview

- Fill `templates/profile.md` and `templates/cv-master.md`; save them (see SKILL.md "Where data
  lives").
- If they had no CV, write the master CV from their answers and show it for approval.
- Run the ATS check (`ats-cv.md`) on the master CV and show the short report.
- Pick regions and source packs (`regions.md`), show the list, and let them remove any.
- Create an empty tracker from `templates/tracker.md`.
- Offer the daily schedule (`scheduling.md`), then run the first search now.
